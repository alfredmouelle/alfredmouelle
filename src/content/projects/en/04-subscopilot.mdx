# SubsCopilot: subscription tracking with WhatsApp and email reminders

## The product

**SubsCopilot** is a web application designed to track recurring subscriptions and automate customer renewal reminders. It lets freelancers and agencies manage subscriptions across multiple client organizations so renewals never slip past unnoticed.

## Key features

- **Centralized dashboard**: track all active recurring services, costs, and renewal dates in one view.
- **Multi-channel alerts**: automated notifications sent through email (Resend) and WhatsApp (Twilio) ahead of payment deadlines.
- **Multi-organization workspaces**: separate client accounts, team members, and services under a single login.
- **Customizable templates**: tailored notification messages adapted to each client or subscription tier.
- **Renewal timelines**: days-remaining counters and next-billing dates updated in real time.

![Subscriptions overview](../_assets/sc-services.webp)

## Client and service separation

SubsCopilot distinguishes internal service costs (tools the agency uses) from client subscriptions (plans billed to clients), making it useful both for tracking company overhead and managing client recurring retainers.

## Pricing tiers

- **Free**: up to 10 services and 2 subscribers per service.
- **Pro**: from 10,000 XAF with unlimited organizations, services, and subscribers.

## Tech stack

- **Frontend & backend**: Next.js App Router with TypeScript.
- **API**: tRPC for type-safe server actions.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL on Neon serverless with Prisma.
- **Authentication**: better-auth.
- **Job scheduling**: Inngest for orchestrating recurring alert cron jobs.
- **Messaging**: Twilio WhatsApp API and Resend with React Email.
- **UI**: Tailwind CSS with Radix UI components.
