# Internal management platform for PINTO Company

## Context

PINTO Company ran an IT service and hardware repair business in Douala. Tracking incoming client devices, diagnostic notes, spare parts, and invoices relied heavily on paper forms and scattered spreadsheets. As repair volume grew, status lookups became slow and error-prone. The management decided to build an internal web application to centralize device workflows under one roof.

## My role

As a web developer, I designed and built the internal management platform across both backend and frontend, focusing on device intake, repair tracking, and inventory.

## What I built

- **Device intake and repair lifecycle**: a module to register customer equipment, record diagnostic findings, assign technicians, track repair stages, and generate pickup receipts.
- **Invoicing and quotes**: automated quote generation calculating spare parts, labor costs, and tax before printing or emailing to clients.
- **Inventory and sales**: tracking parts stock, low-inventory alerts, and refurbished device sales.
- **Database architecture**: modeled the database in UML and implemented the MySQL schema with foreign key constraints, indexes, and stored procedures for complex reporting queries.
- **Responsive frontend**: built the UI using Bootstrap, jQuery, and Ajax to submit repair updates and search equipment without reloading the page.

## Key takeaways

The primary challenge was modeling real-world repair workflows where hardware moves through unpredictable diagnostic loops and partial part deliveries. This project gave me my first deep experience designing relational schemas for day-to-day operations and building interactive web interfaces around concrete business constraints.
